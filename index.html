ABOUT THE PROJECT

One world. More ways to explore.

Wegoto Travel is an independent travel portal in development. The platform is designed to help travelers discover destinations, find useful travel information and access relevant services through partner integrations. The project is configured for 35 language versions, with content and localization undergoing preparation and review.
